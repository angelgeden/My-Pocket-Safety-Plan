      Passcode

      The passcode is a privacy screen for this device. It keeps casual eyes out. It isn't encryption. Call and text buttons always work on the lock screen.

      Install on your phone

      iPhone / iPad: open this page in Safari, tap Share ⎋, then Add to Home Screen.
      Android: open the browser menu ⋮ and tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
      Computer: in Chrome or Edge, use the install icon in the address bar.

      Once installed it opens like an app and works offline. On iPhone and iPad, installing also protects your answers from being cleared after a week of not visiting.

      Moving to a new device

      Tap Back up here to save a file. On the other device, open the app and tap Restore. You can merge the two so nothing is lost.

      About

      My Pocket Safety Plan is a personal planning tool, not an emergency service or medical advice. It works best alongside care from a clinician and the people you trust. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number.
